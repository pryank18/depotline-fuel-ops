# Depotline — Market Requirements Document

## Market problem

Small and mid-size fuel distribution operations are a large, highly fragmented segment that mostly runs on manual tooling: a stock spreadsheet, a separate pricing sheet, and a paper notebook or WhatsApp thread for fleet fuel logs. None of these tools talk to each other, so operators reconcile three sources by hand — usually only at month-end, by which point pricing has already been set blind to current stock and fuel losses have already happened.

## Target market

Independent and small-chain fuel depot or distribution operators — typically one or two people handling stock, pricing, and fleet oversight without a dedicated ops team or an enterprise ERP budget.

## Why now

Enterprise fuel/fleet management software is built and priced for large, multi-site operators, leaving small operators to either overpay for enterprise tooling or continue on spreadsheets and paper. There is a gap for a lightweight, single-site tool scoped to what a one- or two-person team actually needs day to day.

## Competitive landscape

- **Enterprise fuel/fleet ERP suites** — feature-complete but priced and scoped for multi-site operations; overkill and often unaffordable for a single depot
- **Generic spreadsheet templates** — free and flexible but require manual reconciliation across separate stock, pricing, and fleet-log sheets
- **Paper / WhatsApp logs** — the default fallback for fleet fuel tracking; effectively unauditable

## Value proposition

Depotline consolidates stock, pricing, and fleet fuel logs into one dashboard, purpose-built for the scale of a single depot — giving an operator a live margin number and an auditable fleet log without the cost or complexity of enterprise tooling.

## Positioning

A single-site, single-screen alternative to spreadsheets and paper logs — not a competitor to enterprise fuel ERP, but a fit for the operators enterprise tools don't serve.

## Status

Live, single-site v1. Multi-site/multi-depot rollups are a candidate for a future release once the single-site workflow is validated.
